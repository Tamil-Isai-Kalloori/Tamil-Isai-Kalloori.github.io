Tamil Isai Perarignar Award

Every year on the opening day of the Tamil Isai Vizha, the Sangam confers the title “Tamil Isai Perarignar” (தமிழ் இசைப்பேரறிஞர் — Great Scholar of Tamil Music) upon an eminent musician, accompanied by a gold medal (பொற்பதக்கம்) and a cash prize (பொன்முடிப்பு). This honour has been bestowed annually since 1957.

Notable recipients include Semmangudi Srinivasa Iyer (1969), M.S. Subbulakshmi (1970), D.K. Pattammal (1973), T. Balasaraswati (1975), M.L. Vasanthakumari (1978), Seerkazhi S. Govindarajan (1982), and Lalgudi G. Jayaraman (1984).

Pann Isai Arignar Award

Since 2009, a second award — “Pann Isai Arignar” (பண் இசை அறிஞர் — Scholar of Pann Music) — has also been conferred. This award recognises Odhuvars (ஓதுவார் — temple musicians who sing Thevaram in the ancient Pann tradition) and researchers who have dedicated their lives to preserving and promoting the ancient Pann musical heritage.
