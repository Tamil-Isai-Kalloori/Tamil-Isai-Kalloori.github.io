Chief Guests

Special guests of honour at the Isai Vizha since 1943.

  1. Home
  2. Isai Vizha

The Founding and Its Patrons

The revival of Tamil music owes its modern impetus to Raja Sir Annamalai Chettiar, who recognised that Tamil Isai — despite a lineage stretching back millennia — had been eclipsed over several centuries. His founding of the Tamil Isai Sangam gave the movement its institutional home.

He was supported by an extraordinary group of Tamil scholars and public figures: Dr. R.K. Shanmugam Chettiar, Kalki Krishnamurthy, C. Rajagopalachari, and Perarignar C.N. Annadurai, whose advocacy gave the Sangam both cultural authority and public visibility.

Government Recognition

The Sangam’s standing is attested by the continuing support of the Chennai Iyal Isai Nadaga Mandram (under the Government of Tamil Nadu), the Sangeet Natak Akademi (New Delhi), and the Ministry of Culture of the Government of India.

Distinguished Visitors

The Tamil Isai Vizha has been graced by heads of state, chief ministers, governors, judges, and eminent cultural figures since its very first edition in 1943. Their participation is a testament to the stature and national significance of Tamil Isai Sangam’s mission.

Notable chief guests have included Presidents of India Rajendra Prasad, S. Radhakrishnan, Zakir Hussain, and A.P.J. Abdul Kalam; Chief Ministers M.G. Ramachandran, M. Karunanidhi, and J. Jayalalithaa; as well as many Governors, Union Ministers, Supreme Court Justices, and cultural luminaries over eight decades.
