Isai Vizha

The annual Margazhi Tamil Music Festival of Tamil Isai Sangam.

  1. Home
  2. Isai Vizha

The Tamil Isai Vizha (தமிழிசை விழா) is a twelve-day music festival held every December, running continuously since the Sangam’s founding in 1943. It is one of Chennai’s landmark cultural events during the Margazhi season — the Tamil month (mid-December to mid-January) traditionally associated with devotional music and the performing arts.

Highlight Events

  • Pann Research Conference: Held December 22–25 every year since 1949, this scholarly forum brings together Odhuvars (temple musicians), musicologists, Tamil scholars, and researchers to map the ancient Tamil Panns mentioned in Silappadikaram, Thevaram, and Divyaprabandham to their modern Carnatic raga equivalents. To date, 97 Pann–raga correspondences have been formally announced.

  • Tamil Isai Perarignar Award: Conferred annually since 1957, this prestigious title is awarded to an eminent musician on the festival’s opening day, along with a gold medal and a cash prize. Past recipients include M.S. Subbulakshmi (1970), Semmangudi Srinivasa Iyer (1969), D.K. Pattammal (1973), T. Balasaraswati (1975), Lalgudi G. Jayaraman (1984), M.L. Vasanthakumari (1978), and Seerkazhi S. Govindarajan (1982). A second award, “Pann Isai Arignar,” has been conferred since 2009.

  • Chief Guests of Honour: The festival has been graced by Presidents of India (Rajendra Prasad, S. Radhakrishnan, Zakir Hussain, A.P.J. Abdul Kalam), Chief Ministers, Governors, and other luminaries over its eight decades.

Recognition

The festival enjoys recognition and support from the Chennai Iyal Isai Nadaga Mandram (Tamil Nadu Government), the Sangeet Natak Akademi (New Delhi), and the Ministry of Culture (Government of India).
