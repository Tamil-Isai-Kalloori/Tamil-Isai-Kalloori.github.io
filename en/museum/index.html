Thol Isai Kalanjiyam

Thol Isai Kalanjiyam — a collection of ancient and rare musical instruments from across India.

Thol Isai Kalanjiyam (Traditional Musical Instruments Museum) houses a curated collection of ancient and rare musical instruments gathered from across India.

Inaugurated: February 2019, by the Governor of Tamil Nadu.

Location: Tamil Isai Sangam, 2nd Floor, Raja Annamalai Mandram, Chennai – 600 108.

Visiting hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed on Mondays.

Admission:

  • General: ₹20 per person
  • Students below age 21: Free (valid ID required)
  • Senior citizens: Free (valid ID required)
  • Educational institutions: ₹300 (institutional fee)
  • Foreign visitors: ₹200

Wheelchair assistance is available.

Curator: Dr. Lalitha Jawahar — +91 96001 29724

Contact: +91 44 2534 1425 / +91 44 2533 0350

The Collection

The museum displays ancient and rare musical instruments from across India, spanning string, wind, and percussion families. The live site shows 18 exhibit photographs but does not publish a formal catalogue or inventory.

Visit

Thol Isai Kalanjiyam welcomes you all to this fascinating experience! Come and discover the ancient musical instruments of India.
