Tamil Isai Sangam holds concerts, workshops, competitions and conferences throughout the year. Its flagship event is the 12-day Tamil Isai Vizha every December.

See Upcoming events for what is next, and Past events for what has already taken place. An event moves to the past list automatically once it is over.

To learn more about the annual festival, visit the Isai Vizha page.
