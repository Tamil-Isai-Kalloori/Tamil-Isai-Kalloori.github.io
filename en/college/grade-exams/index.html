Grade Examinations

TISGEC — Tamil Isai Sangam Grade Examination Centre: 8 grades across 9 disciplines.

The Tamil Isai Sangam Grade Examination Centre (TISGEC) conducts graded examinations across 8 levels, each spanning 6 months. Exams are conducted once every 6 months. A student may attempt only one grade at a time and must pass the previous level before advancing.

Disciplines

Vocal · Veena · Violin · Flute · Mridangam · Tavil · Nagaswaram · Bharatanatyam · Folk

Age Requirements

  • Music (all instruments and vocal): Completion of 7 years on the date of application
  • Bharatanatyam: Completion of 8 years on the date of application

Examination Format

  • Grades 1–3: Practical exams only
  • Grades 4–8: Practical and theory
  • Only Tamil compositions may be performed

Teacher / Institution Registration

All private music/dance teachers and institutions must register with TISGEC and obtain a registration number for each grade every six months.

Key Rules

  1. Syllabus is issued by Tamil Isai Sangam for each grade.
  2. Tamil Isai Sangam does not conduct training classes — training is the teacher’s responsibility.
  3. Teachers must register first; students can then be enrolled for exams.
  4. Certificates are issued by Tamil Isai Sangam upon successful completion of each grade.
  5. The teacher/faculty must accompany Bharatanatyam students to perform Nattuvangam during the exam.
