Tamil Isai Sangam — photo gallery of Isai Vizha award recipients.

  1. Home
  2. Gallery

A curated collection of photographs celebrating the Carnatic music and Bharatanatyam traditions honoured by Tamil Isai Sangam.

Important note: The photographs in these albums are representative, openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons — they are not the institution’s own archive of named award recipients. The original five award albums (Deiviga Arulalarkal, Isai Arulalarkal, Isai Kalaivanarkal, Isai Perarignarkal, Pann Isai Perarignarkal) contain 126 portraits owned by Tamil Isai Sangam which are pending supply for the new website.

Carnatic Vocal Concerts

Representative photographs of Carnatic vocal concerts — these are openly licensed performance photographs from Wikimedia Commons, not the institution's own archive. They illustrate the concert traditions celebrated at the Isai Vizha.

Carnatic Instrumentalists

Representative photographs of Carnatic instrumental performers — openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons illustrating the violin, mridangam, and ghatam traditions central to Tamil music.

Bharatanatyam Performances

Representative photographs of Bharatanatyam dance — openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons. These illustrate the classical dance tradition honoured at the Isai Vizha, not specific awardees of Tamil Isai Sangam.

Legendary Performers

Portraits and concert photographs of renowned Carnatic musicians — openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons. These are representative of the calibre of artists honoured by Tamil Isai Sangam, not the institution's own portrait archive.

Award Recipients Gallery
