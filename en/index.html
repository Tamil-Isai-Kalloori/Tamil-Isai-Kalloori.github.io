A Predominant Place for Tamil Songs

Tamil Isai Sangam was founded in 1943 by Raja Sir Annamalai Chettiar to give Tamil songs a predominant place in music concerts — ending their relegation as “tail-enders” in Carnatic programmes. The founding was supported by India’s first Finance Minister Dr R.K. Shanmugam Chettiar, the first Indian Governor General C. Rajagopalachari, Kalki Krishnamoorthy, T.K. Chidambaranathan Mudaliar, C.N. Annadurai, and others.

Tamil Isai Kalloori (the college) began in 1944 as an evening college and expanded to a full-time day college in 1995. Affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Music and Fine Arts University, it offers undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, diplomas, and certificates in Carnatic music, instruments, and Bharatanatyam.

Every December the Sangam holds the Isai Vizha — a 12-day festival of concerts, the Isai Perarignar award, and the Pann research conference that has been mapping ancient Tamil Panns to modern ragas since 1949. In 2019, the Thol Isai Kalanjiyam, a museum of ancient musical instruments, was inaugurated at the Raja Annamalai Mandram.
