Admissions

Tamil Isai Kalloori admissions — courses, fees, eligibility, and how to apply.

Enquire→

Tamil Isai Kalloori offers a range of programmes in vocal music, instrumental music, and Bharatanatyam. Degree courses, diplomas, certificates, grade examinations, online classes, and distance education — all routes are described below.

Day College (10 AM – 4 PM)

Affiliated to Tamil Nadu Music and Fine Arts University. No age bar. Scholarships available for Nadaswaram and Tavil students.

Undergraduate Course Application (Google Form)

Diploma Course Application (Google Form)

Certificate Course Application (Google Form)

Special Course Application (Google Form)

Evening College (5:00 PM – 7:15 PM)

Mid-June through April 30. Certificate courses — individual application required.

TISGEC Grade Examinations

Tamil Isai Sangam Grade Exam Centre (TISGEC) — open to school-going children and external students. Eight grade levels, six months each. Minimum age: 7 (music), 8 (Bharatanatyam). Only Tamil compositions to be performed.

Online / Skype Classes

1 year / 40 classes, each 1 hour. An ‘Isai Kalai Certificate’ is issued upon successful completion.

CategoryFee

Online class registration is not open yet; please contact the office for details.

Distance Education (Tamil University, Thanjavur)

Since 2016, Tamil Isai Kalloori has functioned as a study centre of Tamil University’s Distance Education programme. UGC approved.

Application: send a DD for ₹200/- to “The Co-ordinator, Tamil Isai Sangam, Chennai – 600 108”, or submit in person at the college office.

A downloadable application form is not yet available online; please contact the office.
